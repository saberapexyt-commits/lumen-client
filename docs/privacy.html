Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 19, 2026

Lumen Client runs entirely on your computer. The developer operates no server for it and collects no analytics, telemetry or personal data.

Microsoft sign-in

You sign in on Microsoft's own page (device-code flow); Lumen never sees or stores your password. Lumen requests the XboxLive.signin and offline_access permissions, uses them to obtain a Minecraft access token so you can launch the game you own, and reads your Minecraft profile name and ID to use in the game. Your Microsoft refresh token is stored only on your computer, encrypted with your operating system's keychain. You can remove it any time with Sign out in Settings, or revoke the app's access in your Microsoft account settings.

What stays on your computer

Profiles, mods, settings, logs and the encrypted token live in your user data folder (on Windows, %APPDATA%\LumenClient). Logs do not contain your access token.

Network connections

Lumen contacts these services directly from your computer, only as needed for the feature you use: Microsoft, Xbox Live and Minecraft services (sign-in); Mojang (game files, Java runtimes, news); Modrinth and CurseForge (mod search and downloads; a CurseForge API key you add is sent only to CurseForge); Fabric, Quilt, Forge and NeoForge servers (loader files); and GitHub (checking for launcher updates). Those services receive your IP address like any website would and have their own privacy policies. Nothing is sent to the developer.

Children

Lumen does not knowingly collect data from anyone, including children. Minecraft accounts for children are managed by Microsoft Family.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the date above will change. Questions: open an issue on the GitHub repository.
