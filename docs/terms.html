1. What Lumen Client is

Lumen Client ("Lumen") is a free, open-source launcher for Minecraft: Java Edition. It downloads game files from Mojang's servers onto your computer, helps you install mods from third-party sources, and starts the game. It is made by an independent developer and is not an official Minecraft product.

2. You need your own copy of Minecraft

Lumen does not include or sell Minecraft. To play you must own Minecraft: Java Edition and sign in with the Microsoft account that owns it. Lumen does not provide, and will not help you obtain, pirated or unlicensed copies of the game.

Lumen downloads them only through those services' official APIs, when you ask, and respects authors' settings. For example, mods that disallow third-party downloads must be downloaded manually from the source.

4. Use at your own risk

Mods are written by third parties and can crash the game, corrupt worlds or contain bugs. Lumen checks version and loader compatibility and keeps removed mods in a trash folder, but it cannot guarantee that any mod is safe or works. Back up worlds you care about.
